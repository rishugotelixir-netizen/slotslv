Workshops

Learn alongside someone who does this every week. Small groups, real tools, finished projects.

Weekend Woodworking

A two-day introduction to hand tools and machine basics, ending with a small box or stool you take home. All materials provided; wear clothes you do not mind marking.

Repair Before Replace

Practical fixes for wobbly chairs, loose joints, torn upholstery, and small appliances that still work if you open them up. Bring the broken item.

Textiles & Simple Sewing

Reading patterns, choosing fabric, and machine basics. We cover repairs and alterations first so you can extend the life of what you already own.

Custom Group Sessions

Team building, school groups, or community classes. We design a session around your level and the projects you actually want to finish.

How a Session Works

Every workshop follows the same structure so you always know what to expect before you arrive.

  • Short intro: Tools, materials, and safety briefing before anything is powered on.
  • Demonstration first: We build one sample piece together, so you see the correct technique before you try it.
  • Hands-on time: The majority of the session is you working, with one instructor moving between tables.
  • Finish and take home: You leave with the finished piece, a printed guide, and a list of the tools worth buying next.
