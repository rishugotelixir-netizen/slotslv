FAQ

Common questions about our guides, workshops, and materials.

What is DIY & Crafts?

We publish step-by-step project guides for home makers — woodworking, textiles, repairs, and general craft — with materials lists, honest time estimates, and the mistakes we actually made while testing each build.

Do I need expensive tools?

No. Most guides are written to work with basic hand tools and can be completed in a home workspace. Where a project genuinely needs a machine tool, we label it as an advanced project and suggest a cheaper alternative where one exists.

How do I know the time estimates are real?

Every estimate is the recorded elapsed time from our own test build, including setup and finishing, not just the cutting and assembly. Add an extra evening if you are new to the tools involved.

Where do I buy the materials?

We list materials by type and specification rather than by store, so you can shop at any hardware store, timber yard, or craft supplier. Where a material is unusual, we note the common substitutes.

Are the guides free?

Yes. Every project guide on the site is free to read, with no account required. Workshops are paid because they consume materials and instructor time.

Can I suggest a project or report an error?

Yes, and we would rather have the correction. Use the contact form on the home page with the guide name and what you found, and we will update the guide and note the revision.

Do you sell materials or finished products?

No. We are a content and education site, not a shop. We do not take payment for recommendations, so the guides reflect what we would buy ourselves.

What safety gear do I need?

Every guide lists required protective equipment at the top. As a baseline, keep safety glasses within reach, wear closed shoes, keep the work area ventilated, and disconnect power tools before changing a blade or bit.
