Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-08-22

Overview

DIY & Crafts ("we", "us", "our") is a project guide and workshop website. This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you use the site (the "Service"). We do not require an account and we deliberately collect as little as possible.

Information We Handle

  • Local Data: The project idea browser saves your saved builds and preferences in your browser's localStorage. This never leaves your device and is not sent to us.
  • Contact Information: If you use the contact form, we receive the name, email address, and message you submit so we can reply.
  • Workshop Enquiries: If you email us about a workshop, we keep your message to answer the enquiry and arrange dates.

Data Security

No online service is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect information you send us from loss, theft, and unauthorised access. Because most site data stays in your browser, our exposure is limited.

Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to anyone under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. Functional storage such as sessionStorage may be used to remember whether an on-screen prompt has already been dismissed during a visit, and it clears when you close the tab.

Your Choices

  • You can clear saved builds and preferences at any time through your browser's site data settings.
  • You can ask us to delete any message you have sent us by contacting us.
  • You can ask what information we hold about you, and we will respond.

Third-Party Links

Some guides link to manufacturer documentation or supplier pages. Those sites are governed by their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will revise the date at the top of this page. Material changes will be noted on the site.
